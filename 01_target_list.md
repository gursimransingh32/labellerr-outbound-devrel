# 1. Target companies and contacts

20 companies in 5 segments, ranked by `src/outbound.py` (as of 2026-09-28). Each has two contacts: the technical buyer first, then an exec or intro route. Contacts marked *confirm* could not be fully verified from a public source and must be checked on LinkedIn before sending.

## Verifying that no target is already a Labellerr customer

- **Case-study page:** labellerr.com/case-studies lists 16 named customers; one more (Mythos AI) appears in a blog case study. Three older case studies do not name the customer (a non-profit research institute, a packaged-food supplier and a beauty-tech startup); none of the 20 targets fits those descriptions.
- **Logo wall (checked by eye):** Almost every logo on labellerr.com/robotics-data has the alt text "university of maryland", so a script cannot read it. Checked visually, it shows 14 logos: Spare-it, Adobe, University of Maryland, MIT, FOSS, Mythos AI, Spot AI, Kapsys, Oishii, Aftershoot, Volvo, Krevera, Coupang and Public Record Office Victoria. MIT and Kapsys appear nowhere else on Labellerr's site, so a text-only check would have missed them.
- **LinkedIn tagline:** Labellerr's LinkedIn page says it is trusted by Stanford, UC Davis, Volvo, Coupang and Toyota AI. UC Davis and Toyota AI have no case study, so the page alone would have missed them.
- **Partners and competitors:** Labellerr sells through the Siemens Xcelerator Marketplace (partner, excluded). Companies featured in Labellerr's own comparison blogs (Scale AI, Encord and similar) are competitors, not targets.
- **Customers' brand families:** A brand owned by a customer is not a clean cold target either, so I mapped them and hold them for review: Zenseact (Volvo Cars); Mack Trucks, Renault Trucks and Volvo Autonomous Solutions (Volvo Group); Farfetch (Coupang); Tortuga AgTech, the harvest-robot maker Oishii bought in 2025; Vanderlande and Bastian Solutions (Toyota Industries); Altair (Siemens). 37 brand names in total.

All 20 targets come back CLEAR against 24 customers/partners and 37 owned brands. Because Toyota AI is a customer, I block Toyota's AI arms (e.g. Toyota Research Institute) outright and flag any other Toyota entity for review; that is why the script matches on distinctive words, not just exact names. On a normal machine, bash run.sh re-reads the live pages, so customers added after today are caught too.

Full do-not-contact snapshot (24 customers/partners plus 37 owned brands, each with its source): `data/dnc_snapshot.csv`.

## Left out on purpose

- **Toyota Research Institute, Volvo Autonomous Solutions:** Subsidiaries or affiliates of listed customers.
- **Tesla (Optimus):** Runs its own in-house data engine; no realistic buying motion for a 13-person vendor.
- **Defense autonomy (e.g. Saronic):** Data-sovereignty rules make an offshore annotation workforce a hard sell.
- **XDOF:** Sells teleoperation data and annotation to robotics labs, so it is a competitor (or later a partner), not a buyer.
- **Field AI:** Strong fit, but its last public signal is August 2025 and much of its work is defense-adjacent; revisit on the next signal.
